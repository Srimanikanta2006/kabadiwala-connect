## 2. Step-by-Step Demonstration Script

### Demo Step 1: Low-Literacy Vernacular UI & Indic Spoken Audio
- **Goal:** Prove that a non-literate waste-picker can operate the platform without reading English.
- **Action:**
  1. Open [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) in your browser. (Optionally press `F12` and switch to Mobile Viewport — e.g. Samsung Galaxy A12 / Pixel 5).
  2. Tap the top language pill to toggle to **मराठी (MR)** or **हिंदी (HI)**.
  3. Observe **Screen 01 (Home)**: all market rates are displayed in Devanagari script (`PCB ₹780`, `केबल ₹420`, `बॅटरी ₹110`).
  4. Tap the **Speaker Icon** on any material card.
  5. **Verification:** The device speaks the live rate in native Marathi/Hindi using the Web Speech / Bhashini synthesizer (*"छापील सर्किट बोर्ड: ७८० रुपये प्रति किलो"*).

---

### Demo Step 2: Camera Capture & MobileNetV2 Vision AI
- **Goal:** Prove edge-tolerant automated scrap classification.
- **Action:**
  1. On **Screen 01 (Home)**, tap the giant green CTA: **"Scan & Identify E-Waste"** (`photo_camera`).
  2. Upload or take a picture of a circuit board (e.g. `stitch-designs/assets/pcb_motherboards.png`).
  3. Observe **Screen 02 (AI Identification)**:
     - The bounding box identifies `Printed Circuit Board (PCB)`.
     - Match confidence displays `92% High` with a bright emerald progress bar.
     - Tap the **Speaker Icon**: hears *"इसकी पहचान: हाई-ग्रेड सर्किट बोर्ड (92% निश्चित)।"*
     - Quick weight keypad: Tap `+5kg` or `+1kg` chips to effortlessly adjust weight without manual typing.

---

### Demo Step 3: AI Robustness Fallback (Dim / Cluttered Photos)
- **Goal:** Prove that poor photos do not crash the app or mislead the collector.
- **Action:**
  1. On Screen 02, click **"Retake Photo"** and upload a deliberately dark, blurry, or non-scrap image.
  2. **Verification:**
     - The AI confidence bar drops to `Low (<60%)` and turns amber/red.
     - A prominent amber fallback banner appears:  
       `⚠️ कम रोशनी / अस्पष्ट फोटो - श्रेणी की पुष्टि करें` (*Low Light / Unclear Photo - Please Verify*).
     - Tap **"Select from 6-Category Grid"** or **"Change Category"**.
     - Transitions instantly to **Screen 03 (Category Selection)**: high-contrast 6-tile grid (CRT, LCD, Cables, Batteries, PCB, Motors) with audio pronunciation buttons for manual tap selection.

---

### Demo Step 4: Fair Price Discovery & MCDA Recycler Matching
- **Goal:** Prove transparent market rates and nearest authorized CPCB recycler ranking.
- **Action:**
  1. From Screen 02/03, proceed to **Screen 04 (Price Offers)**.
  2. Observe the dynamic estimated valuation range: `₹8,400 - ₹9,200` for the lot.
  3. Review the top-ranked buyers generated by the Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) engine:
     - **Rank 1:** `EcoRecycle India Pvt Ltd` (1.97 km away, ₹780/kg, Doorstep vehicle pickup available, License: `CPCB/E-WASTE/REG/MH/2023/1042`).
     - **Rank 2:** `GreenCircle Recyclers` (3.42 km away, ₹750/kg).
  4. Tap **"Accept Offer & Generate Receipt"**.

---

### Demo Step 5: Offline-First Architecture & Airplane Mode Simulation
- **Goal:** Prove that the platform functions with zero connectivity and auto-syncs on reconnection.
- **Action:**
  1. In Chrome DevTools, open the **Network** tab and check **Offline** (or enable Airplane Mode on a real mobile device).
  2. Notice the top sync pill turns to **"Offline"** or displays the orange simulator banner.
  3. Create a scrap lot while completely offline.
  4. Notice the sync badge updates: `1 lot waiting to sync`.
  5. The lot is safely stored in **Dexie.js IndexedDB** (`offline_lots`).
  6. Uncheck **Offline** (turn mobile data back on).
  7. **Verification:** The sync engine automatically detects the `online` event, uploads the lot and photo blob to the backend, and the badge turns to green `Synced`.

---

### Demo Step 6: Verifiable Digital Handover QR Receipt
- **Goal:** Prove tamper-proof chain of custody for CPCB EPR audit trails.
- **Action:**
  1. Observe **Screen 05 (Handover Receipt)**.
  2. A live scannable QR code is rendered via `qrcode.react`, encoding the unique token: `KC-TRACE-2026-XXXXXXXX`.
  3. The **4 Pillars of Traceability** checklist shows:
     - ✓ GPS Location Verified (`19.0434° N, 72.8576° E - Dharavi Mandi`)
     - ✓ Scale Photo Stamped
     - ✓ Dual Sign-off Ready
     - ✓ Instant Cash Settlement Flagged
  4. Tap **"Share Receipt"** to demonstrate SMS/WhatsApp link sharing.

---

### Demo Step 7: Authorized Recycler Portal & Weighbridge Confirmation
- **Goal:** Complete the circular loop from the recycler's desktop weighbridge.
- **Action:**
  1. In the top navigation header, toggle the role pill from **Collector** to **Recycler**.
  2. The view switches to the desktop **Recycler Portal (`RecyclerDashboard.jsx`)**.
  3. Logged in as `EcoRecycle India Pvt Ltd` (CPCB Authorized Facility).
  4. Notice the incoming lot from the collector appears in the queue with status **PENDING**.
  5. Click **"Verify & Confirm Handover"**:
     - Enter calibrated weighbridge scale weight (e.g. `12.0 kg`).
     - Select payment method: **CASH CONFIRMED**.
     - Click **"Submit Weighbridge Verification"**.
  6. **Verification:** The system generates an official statutory CPCB certificate:  
     `CPCB-EPR-2026-MH-XXXXXXXX` and updates the transaction status to **CONFIRMED**.

---

### Demo Step 8: Collector Earnings History & Cash Ledger
- **Goal:** Show that the transaction immediately reflects in the collector's financial ledger.
- **Action:**
  1. Switch the role pill back to **Collector**.
  2. Tap the bottom navigation tab: **Earnings (`account_balance_wallet`)**.
  3. Observe **Screen 06 (My Earnings History)**:
     - Monthly earnings total updates to reflect the completed transaction.
     - The transaction list shows the verified lot with a green **PAID (Cash)** badge.
     - The 4-material bento breakdown visualizes shares across PCB, Copper, Battery, and Displays.

## 3. Evaluator Q&A Quick Reference

| Evaluator Question | Engineering Proof in Repository |
| :--- | :--- |
| *How do you ensure informal collectors aren't exploited by digital payment delays?* | The platform enforces **Cash-in-Hand** by default. Digital payments (UPI) are strictly optional. The recycler must hand over physical cash before confirming the weighbridge receipt. |
| *What happens when a collector has no internet in a scrap basement?* | The PWA service worker caches all static assets, and Dexie.js (IndexedDB) stores lots and photo blobs locally. Background auto-sync triggers the moment connectivity resumes (`test_offline_sync.py`). |
| *How does this prevent fraud or duplicate submissions?* | The anomaly engine computes a **64-bit dHash perceptual fingerprint** on every photo ($d \le 4$ flags duplicates) and verifies physical weight density bounds per material (`test_anomaly.py`). |
| *How is the platform financially sustainable?* | Collectors pay **₹0**. Authorized recyclers pay a **2.0% EPR facilitation fee** per verified transaction to obtain audit-ready CPCB credits, generating a **98% gross contribution margin** over serverless compute costs (`docs/field_research.md`). |
